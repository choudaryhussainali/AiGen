Five tools, one tab

Most of them work on material you give them, so the answers follow your syllabus rather than whatever the model happens to remember.

  • Notes summariser

    Upload a lecture PDF and get the main points as bullets. Then ask it anything about the file: it answers from your pages, cites the page numbers, and says when it adds anything from outside them.

  • Exam preparation

    Paste your course outline. AiGen splits it into topics and gives each one a short explanation, what to focus on and the usual mistakes, so you cover what the paper actually asks for instead of guessing.

  • Past paper solver

    Photograph a printed past paper as a JPG or PNG up to 3 MB. Each question is read off the image and worked through step by step, followed by a short explanation of why the method works.

  • Video summariser

    Paste a YouTube link and read the lecture in a few minutes. Its captions are turned into a plain language explanation in the language you pick, so you can decide whether the hour is worth it.

  • Topic explainer

    Type in the concept that won't click, like recursion, entropy or normalisation, and get a plain definition, a real world analogy and three key points.

What happens to a file you upload

Your study material never reaches a hard drive. It lives in server memory for the length of your session and is deleted from it when you log out. Text sent to the AI model for an answer is processed by Groq under its own terms.

Uploaded

PDFs up to 10 MB, images up to 3 MB.

Checked

File type and size confirmed before anything is read.

Read

Text pulled from the PDF, or off the image.

Indexed

Split into chunks the assistant can search.

Answered

Replies built from those chunks, with page numbers.

Wiped

Deleted from memory at logout, or after an idle hour.

What AiGen won't do

Worth knowing before you sign up, so nothing comes as a surprise the night before a paper.

Remember your last session

Log out and the summaries go with it. Copy anything you want to keep before you leave.

Work offline

The models run in the cloud, so a dropped connection stops the assistant mid-answer.

Read messy handwriting

Printed and cleanly scanned papers work best. Cursive notes often come back wrong.

Work in every language

Notes, exam prep and topic answers are built for English. Video summaries can be written in six languages, including Urdu and Hindi.

Open Word or PowerPoint files

PDFs, JPG and PNG images, and YouTube links. Export your slides to PDF first.

Sit your exam for you

Answers come with the working shown, because the point is that you can repeat it.

Bring one PDF and see how far you get

Sign up with your email, upload your hardest set of notes, and log out when you're done. Nothing stays behind.
